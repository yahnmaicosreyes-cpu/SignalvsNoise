Plan Your Week

This planner helps you separate signal (important things that prevent crisis) from noise (things that feel urgent but aren't).

You'll enter your events in four groups, starting with the most important. For each group, add as many events as you like.

  1. Signal — important, not urgent (your foundation)
  2. Urgent — important and urgent (today's fires)
  3. Interruption — urgent but not important
  4. Noise — neither (drop candidates)
